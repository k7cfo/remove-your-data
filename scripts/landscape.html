What this view can tell you

Only known indexed listings in this local log are counted. This is not a map of your complete internet exposure, where your data is stored, or where you live.

Exposed means any listing not recorded as gone, including pending or blocked work. Gone reflects the status recorded in the log, not a new live check. Zero means no listings counted here, not proof of no exposure.

Unknown is a separate bucket for operators without an assigned region. Regions are manually curated; this page performs no geolocation, DNS, WHOIS, or other lookup.

Reading the timeline

History begins when status changes are recorded locally. Older workspaces start with a baseline at migration time; earlier history is not reconstructed. Historical frames use the operator regions assigned now.

Playback steps through recorded timestamps, not equal periods of time. Current is the separate snapshot loaded with this page. The page does not refresh automatically.
