# Erasure / deletion request

Send from the mailbox you want them to reply to. Attach nothing extra. Give them only enough to find the record they already publish.

Subject: Request to delete personal information — [listing URL or broker name]

```text
To the privacy / data-protection team:

I request deletion of my personal information from your website, databases,
and any products, lists, or files you sell, share, or license.

Name as listed: [exactly as shown]
Listing URL: [url]
City / region as listed: [city, region]
Other matching detail already on the listing: [phone last 4 / street — only if shown]

I am the person this record is about. I am providing this information only
to identify the record for deletion.

Please delete the personal information, do not sell or share it, and confirm
in writing to this email. If you refuse, state the exact legal basis.

Legal basis (keep the lines that apply, delete the rest):
- California resident: CCPA/CPRA deletion and, if you are a data broker, the
  Delete Act / DROP obligations.
- Other US: the consumer privacy law of [state].
- EU/EEA: GDPR Article 17 right to erasure.
- UK: UK GDPR right to erasure.
- Elsewhere: your published privacy policy and any applicable local law.

This is not consent to process my data for any other purpose.

Regards,
[name as listed]
```

Log the send time (UTC + local), destination address, and message-id. Start a `legal_response` clock.
